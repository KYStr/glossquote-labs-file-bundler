      Frequently asked questions

      Are my files uploaded?

      No. The candidate processes selected files in the current browser tab and has no account or upload feature. Files and results are not saved in website storage. Clearing this page does not delete downloaded files or securely erase browser memory.

      Will ZIPs meet the limit?

      The tool measures actual ZIP STORE bytes. Source contents are not compressed; names and archive records add overhead. Mail services may add transfer encoding, so leave room for their rules.

      Why are there multiple bundles?

      Files are processed by descending source size, breaking ties by selection order, and placed in the first bundle that fits. Stable first-fit-decreasing accounts for ZIP overhead but does not promise the fewest bundles. It does not split an individual source file into parts.

      Can I cancel or choose files again?

      Yes. Canceling, removing files, changing the limit, or starting over clears old results and temporary URLs. Cancel keeps the file list; Clear all starts empty. Closing the tab or changing language loses undownloaded results.

      Are my originals changed?

      No. Content bytes remain unchanged; ZIP entry names are normalized to NFC. Originals are not deleted or overwritten. The index contains entry names, sizes and CRC32 values; sharing the index also shares those names.
